# Sahara

An original, procedural 360-degree desert world. One imagined dune sea at four hours of a day: first light on the sand, the white noon, a ring of green, and a river of stars.

Open `index.html` through any static HTTP server. No build step. Three.js is vendored locally (MIT license). Fonts have system-serif/sans fallbacks. No analytics, accounts, images or external runtime scripts.

## Interaction

- Drag the sand or use arrow keys to look around.
- Choose an hour with the visible navigation, or swipe within the navigation strip only.
- Tap the caravan, the sandfish or the fennec - or follow the invitation beneath each scene.
- Ambient motion can be paused. Reduced-motion users begin paused.
- Sound is optional and synthesized locally. It starts only after an explicit tap.

This is an artistic interpretation, not a map, travel guide or natural-history report. Sources for the small facts are linked in the notes: Britannica on the Sahara's landforms, NASA Earth Observatory on Saharan dust, and National Geographic on the fennec fox.
